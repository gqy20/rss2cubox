# Product

## Register
product

## Users
People following AI engineering, industry developments and Chinese policy who need to scan daily changes and inspect their evidence.

## Product Purpose
Connect collected articles, AI insights, policy documents, signal clusters and verifiable predictions. Distinguish collection coverage, generated analysis and reviewed outcomes.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, thoughtful, approachable. A personal research journal with useful operational views.

## Anti-references
Heavy blue administration dashboards, permanent three-column information walls, oversized metrics, decorative imagery, and narrow centered pages with wasted margins.

## Design Principles
- Use the approved B ivory/terracotta direction, B1 editorial homepage plus B3 readable article list.
- Reserve Songti for editorial headings; use sans-serif for controls, metadata and lists.
- Give major surfaces soft rounded corners, comfortable internal padding and clear spacing.
- Put evidence next to judgments. Label generated interpretation and unconfirmed policy associations.
- Render actual data and meaningful empty/error states; never fabricate deadlines, counts, accuracy or operational health.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.
